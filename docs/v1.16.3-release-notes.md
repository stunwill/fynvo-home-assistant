# Fynvo v1.16.3

## Accounts & Cards consolidation

Fynvo now treats Accounts and Cards as one coherent management workspace. Accounts remains the primary navigation destination and the workspace switches between Accounts and Cards using a segmented control.

The Cards view is card-first. It lists Card records directly, shows the linked Account as secondary context, and no longer renders empty Account groups for Accounts without Cards.

## Account lifecycle

Accounts can be archived without deleting historical financial data. Archived Accounts are excluded from normal new-record selectors and can be restored later.

Account management also supports dependency inspection and an optional Move eligible records & Archive workflow. Eligible Cards, unprotected Transactions and future Account configuration can be moved to an explicitly selected active destination Account. Historical Scheduled Payments, Transfers and protected Transactions are preserved.

Permanent deletion is available only when no dependencies remain.

## Responsive experience

The Accounts & Cards workspace follows the supplied desktop and mobile mock-ups, with responsive tablet interpolation, compact rows, search/filter controls and mobile-friendly Add actions. Home Assistant ingress visual verification remains a manual acceptance step after CI.
