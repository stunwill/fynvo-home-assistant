# Fynvo v1.16.3 Accounts & Cards acceptance

The supplied desktop, tablet-adaptive and mobile Accounts/Cards mock-ups are the primary visual references for this release.

## Required responsive states

- Desktop Accounts view
- Desktop Cards view
- Tablet Accounts view
- Tablet Cards view
- Mobile Accounts view
- Mobile Cards view
- Mobile Account management/archive flow
- Home Assistant ingress equivalents of the mobile views

## Acceptance principles

- Accounts and Cards share one workspace with an Accounts/Cards segmented control.
- Cards is card-first. Accounts with no cards never appear as empty groups in the Cards view.
- Account rows and Card rows remain compact and avoid the legacy nested panel structure.
- Linked Account information remains secondary to the Card itself.
- Archived Accounts remain visible only when deliberately filtered and are not offered for new Card assignments.
- Account management exposes Edit, Archive Only, Move eligible records & Archive, Restore and safe permanent deletion where allowed.
- Desktop and tablet layouts must not horizontally scroll. Mobile and Home Assistant ingress must fit iPhone-sized viewports without content being obscured by the Tools control.

Installed visual comparison remains a manual acceptance gate because repository CI does not render the Home Assistant ingress shell.
