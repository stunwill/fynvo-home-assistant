# Fynvo v1.16.3 Account relationship safety

Account consolidation distinguishes future configuration from historical financial truth.

## Safe to move

- Cards, when reassigned to an explicitly selected active destination Account.

## Conditionally safe to move

- Transactions that are not protected by transfer/reconciliation relationships and whose Account balance semantics remain compatible.
- Active Recurring Expense Account configuration.
- Income destination Account configuration.
- Bill Account configuration.
- Planned Spending Account configuration.

Transactions are preserved rather than moved when the source Account has an opening balance or when protected historical Transactions are present. Transaction reassignment between asset and liability Accounts is rejected because stored balance directions differ.

## Historical / preserve

- Scheduled Payments.
- Transfers and their source/destination Account relationships.
- Transactions protected by Transfers or matched Scheduled Payments.

Move & Archive is executed transactionally. Any failure rolls the operation back before the source Account is archived.
