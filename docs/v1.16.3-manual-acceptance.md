# v1.16.3 manual acceptance scenarios

After CI passes, verify in the installed Home Assistant add-on:

1. Accounts opens the combined Accounts & Cards workspace.
2. Cards is not a separate top-level menu item.
3. Accounts/Cards toggle switches views without a full reload.
4. Cards view lists only Cards and shows each linked Account.
5. Active Account management exposes Edit, Archive Only and Move records & Archive.
6. Archived Accounts appear only when filtered and can be restored.
7. Add/Edit Card excludes archived Accounts.
8. Desktop, tablet, iPhone and ingress layouts do not horizontally scroll or collide with Tools.
